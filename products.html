Quality Assurance

Checked at every stage, not just at the end

Our rigorous quality control systems ensure that every bale of tobacco meets international standards. From field monitoring to final processing, we maintain strict quality checks at every stage, guaranteeing consistency and excellence in every shipment.

  • Field monitoring by our agronomy team through the growing and curing season.
  • Complete traceability from farm to export, documented at every step.
  • Compliance with international tobacco regulations, with responsible growing verified.
  • Packed to the customer's specification rather than to a single house standard.
