Our approach

Quality begins at the farm

Through our contract farming system we work closely with tobacco growers across Zimbabwe's premier growing regions, providing the technical expertise, inputs and support they need to produce world-class leaf.

  • Qualified agronomists supporting contracted farmers through the whole growing season.
  • Inputs and technical support supplied against the contract, so growers are not financing the crop alone.
  • Investment in farming communities through education, infrastructure and sustainable agriculture initiatives.

Vision & Mission

Built to last longer than one good season

Our vision is to be recognised as the leading tobacco leaf merchant in Zimbabwe — setting the benchmark for quality, sustainable farming practices and farmer partnerships while delivering exceptional value to our international customers.

  • Quality first. Flue-cured Virginia that meets and exceeds international standards and our customers' specific blend requirements.
  • Farmer partnership. Agronomic support, fair pricing and access to quality inputs, so growers can improve their livelihoods.
  • Sustainable practices. Afforestation programmes, efficient curing systems and soil conservation that protect Zimbabwe's agricultural future.
